What we created

Day 2 is the new work: a public-roads gravel return from Camping Villette to Sherbrooke that replaces the mountain crossing. From Villette the line follows Chemin Lebel, Chemin Saint-Denis, and Chemin Saint-Jacques through St-Herménégilde, then farm gravel via Chemin Scalabrini and Chemin de la Rivière into Sainte-Edwidge-de-Clifton, continues on Chemin de Clifton toward the Eaton corridor, picks up Rue Principale Ouest in Martinville, and returns through Chemin Orr (Milby / Waterville) into Lennoxville and Sherbrooke.

That corridor deliberately skips Mount Hereford summit, the Circuits Frontières singletrack stack (Roch Voisine, JDA, Chaînon Manquant, Bobines, La Grange, and related trails), and the disputed Hereford Community Forest connectors on Lépine, Sam’s Turnpike, and Eaton–Beloin. Max elevation on Day 2 is about 504 m versus about 722 m on the old gravel summit approach. We did not invent private forest shortcuts; the geometry stays on named municipal and numbered public roads. If a “chemin privé / interdit” sign appears in the field, turn onto the next public numbered road (QC-141 / QC-147 / QC-253 corridors) and do not trespass.

Created segment focus: Villette → St-Herménégilde → Ste-Edwidge → Martinville → Waterville → Sherbrooke. Day 1 largely reuses the classic Sherbrooke–North Hatley–Hatley–Coaticook–Villette public corridor; Day 2 is the redesign.

Camp and access

Camping Villette is the preferred overnight (94 chemin Jean-Paul-Dupont, Saint-Herménégilde). Season listings cover mid-May through mid-October 2026, so Sep 21 is inside the published window. Call to confirm vacancy; this brief does not book anything. Phone 819 849-6273 · campingvillette.com. Plan B if full: Parc de la Gorge de Coaticook camping on the Day 1 corridor.

Access: car is usually preferred from Outremont to Sherbrooke (about 2h15). REM is available as a rule when it fits the schedule and bike policy, but for this start car is the usual choice. Start/finish pin is near Place de la Gare / Mont-Bellevue area in Sherbrooke.

Access ethics and hunting week. Zone 6 small-game season opens 19 Sep 2026, so Sep 21–22 are early hunting days. Our bypass lowers forest exposure versus Eaton–Beloin / summit tracks, but wear bright colors and assume hunters on adjacent land. Do not ride gated or posted private forest roads. Do not invent shortcuts through Hereford Community Forest.

Honest tradeoffs

  • Lose Mount Hereford summit views and the mountain character of the Express.
  • Gain a true ~32 mm+ gravel overnight on public roads, closer to the original easier difficulty spirit.
  • Our loop is shorter (~139 km vs ~165 km on this GPX) with less ascent (~1283 m vs ~1830 m).
  • Respects the Oct 2024 landowner / access constraints that pushed the published route onto Circuits Frontières singletrack.

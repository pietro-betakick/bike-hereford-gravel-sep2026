## What we created (Day 2 bypass)

The created segment is a **municipal public-roads gravel return** that replaces the mountain crossing:

1. **Chemin Lebel** (St-Herménégilde)  
2. **Chemin Saint-Denis**  
3. **Chemin Saint-Jacques**  
4. **Chemin Scalabrini** → **Chemin de la Rivière** (Sainte-Edwidge-de-Clifton)  
5. **Chemin de Clifton** (toward Saint-Isidore / Eaton)  
6. **Rue Principale Ouest** (Martinville)  
7. **Chemin Orr** (Milby / Waterville)  
8. Lennoxville links → **Sherbrooke**

**Intentionally out:** Mount Hereford summit; Circuits Frontières singletrack (Roch Voisine, JDA, Chaînon Manquant, Bobines, La Grange, related); disputed **Lépine / Sam’s Turnpike / Eaton–Beloin** forest connectors. Max Day 2 elev ≈ **504 m**. Geometry stays on named public ways; if a “chemin privé / interdit” sign appears, use the next numbered public road (QC-141 / QC-147 / QC-253) and do not trespass.

## Access and camp

- **Camp:** Camping Villette preferred (season window covers Sep 21 2026; call to confirm; do not book from this doc). Plan B: Parc de la Gorge de Coaticook.  
- **Access:** car usually preferred Outremont → Sherbrooke (~2h15). REM is available as a rule when it fits, but car is usual for this start.

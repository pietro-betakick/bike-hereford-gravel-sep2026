## Itinerary

**Mon 21 — Sherbrooke → Camping Villette (~74 km)**  
North Hatley → Hatley → Coaticook → camp. Resupply in North Hatley and Coaticook.

**Tue 22 — Villette → Sherbrooke (~58 km)**  
St-Herménégilde (Lebel / Saint-Denis / Saint-Jacques / Route 251) → Sainte-Edwidge-de-Clifton → Martinville → Waterville (Chemin Orr) → Lennoxville → Sherbrooke.

Day 2 skips Mount Hereford, Circuits Frontières trails, and the Lépine / Sam’s Turnpike / Eaton–Beloin forest connectors. If a “chemin privé / interdit” sign appears, use the next numbered public road (QC-141, 147, 251, or 253).

The published Hereford Express (Oct 2024) uses ~15 km of Circuits Frontières MTB singletrack over Mount Hereford (difficulty 7/10). This rewrite keeps the counterclockwise town-to-camp day, then returns on public gravel and paved roads through St-Herménégilde, Ste-Edwidge, Martinville, and Waterville. Summit and Lépine / Sam’s Turnpike / Eaton–Beloin forest connectors are skipped on purpose (land-access conflict).
Weather (fetched 17 Sep · MET Norway). Cool and mostly dry on both days. Mon along Coaticook / Sherbrooke: about 8–14°C, cloudy to partly cloudy, ~0 mm daytime, light wind. Tue: clearer, about 7–15°C, dry. Cold mornings (~7–9°C at 8:00). Recheck Sunday night. Small-game season in zone 6 opened Sep 19 — wear bright colors on wooded farm roads even though this bypass cuts the deepest Hereford forest exposure.

Day 1 · Mon Sep 21

Drive Outremont → Sherbrooke (~2 h 15). Roll counterclockwise through North Hatley and Hatley into Coaticook, then to camp. Easier town day; save legs for Tuesday’s longer gravel.

  • Stops: North Hatley bakery; Coaticook (Hop Station, Laiterie de Coaticook).
  • Surfaces: bike paths, pavement, easy gravel. No mountain.

Night · Camping Villette

Pick: Camping VilletteIn season

Family campground · cyclist-friendly (often OK without reservation). Season cited May 15 – Oct 15 2026. Call 819 849-6273 to confirm Mon 21. Nothing booked from here.

  • Plan B: Camping de la Gorge de Coaticook (Bienvenue cyclistes).
  • Mont Expérience Hereford is more useful if you were doing the summit climb (we are not).

Day 2 · Tue Sep 22 · gravel bypass

From Villette north on Chemin Lebel → Saint-Denis → Saint-Jacques → Scalabrini / Rivière (Ste-Edwidge) → Clifton → Martinville → Orr (Waterville) → Sherbrooke. Carry water; services thin until Martinville / town.

If you hit a “chemin privé” or gate, do not trespass. Drop to the next public numbered road (QC-141 / 147 / 253) and continue.

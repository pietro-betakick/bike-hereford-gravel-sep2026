Day 2 corridor

From Camping Villette back to Sherbrooke on public roads:

  • Chemin Lebel → Chemin Saint-Denis → Chemin Saint-Jacques → Route 251 through St-Herménégilde
  • Sainte-Edwidge-de-Clifton → Martinville → Chemin Orr (Waterville / Milby)
  • Into Lennoxville on Rue Winder / Rue Queen (QC-143), then Sherbrooke

Skipped on purpose: Mount Hereford, Circuits Frontières trails, and the Lépine / Sam’s Turnpike / Eaton–Beloin forest connectors. If you see a “chemin privé / interdit” sign, turn onto the next numbered public road (QC-141, 147, 251, or 253).

Camp and getting there

Camping Villette — 94 chemin Jean-Paul-Dupont, Saint-Herménégilde. Season runs mid-May through mid-October 2026, so Sep 21 is inside the window. Call to confirm a site: 819 849-6273 · campingvillette.com. Backup if full: camping at Parc de la Gorge de Coaticook on the Day 1 corridor.

Getting there: drive Outremont → Sherbrooke, about 2h15. Start and finish near Place de la Gare / Mont-Bellevue in Sherbrooke.

Hunting. Zone 6 small-game season opens 19 Sep 2026, so Mon–Tue are early hunting days. Wear bright colours and assume hunters on adjacent land.

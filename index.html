Legal standard for this brief. The routed line uses OSM public highways: primary / secondary / tertiary / unclassified / residential, plus signed cycleways and a few hundred metres of asphalt path tagged bicycle=designated. Mount Hereford singletrack, Lépine, Sam’s Turnpike, and Eaton–Beloin forest connectors are out. If you ever see a “chemin privé / interdit” sign in the field, turn onto the next numbered public road. Do not cut through.

Day 2 · Tue Sep 22 · detail

Break camp early. North on public farm roads through St-Herménégilde / Ste-Edwidge, then Martinville–Waterville corridors back into Sherbrooke. Carry water; services are thinner than Day 1.

Stops

  • Dépanneur Ultra — 174 Chemin Favreau, Sainte-Edwidge-de-Clifton · water / snacks mid-morning.
  • Martinville / Waterville: limited cafés; plan main lunch earlier or finish into Sherbrooke.
  • Sherbrooke: many options once you hit town bike paths again.
